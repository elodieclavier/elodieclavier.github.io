Elodie Clavier

Elodie is an illustrator originally from Nantes, France.
After studying Applied Arts, she chose to specialize in illustration, developing a timeless style focused on the pursuit of aesthetics and elegance. This artistic approach allows her to collaborate regularly with prestigious Houses such as Chanel, Le Ritz, and Montblanc.

Gradually, delicate backgrounds have come to enrich her minimalist lines, creating beautiful scenes of life that invite you to travel and unwind!

Elodie draws with ink or on a tablet and also performs live drawing events, where she sketches graceful and feminine silhouettes in real time.
